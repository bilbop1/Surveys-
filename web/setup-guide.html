StudyFlow Control Center
The Setup Guide

One read, start to finish, and your paid-research operation is live: a dedicated inbox feeding a private dashboard, reachable from every device you own, with zero terms-of-service risk.

◎ How the machine fits together

Five pieces. Each one is boring on its own — together they remove every excuse between you and a booked session.

  1. A dedicated Gmail account — every research platform points its notifications here. One inbox = one source of truth.
  2. The five platforms — Respondent, User Interviews, UserTesting, Prolific, dscout — each tuned to email you the moment a study matches you.
  3. StudyFlow — your private dashboard. It reads that inbox only (your own mail, nothing scraped), parses every invite into a ranked offer card, tracks your pipeline, logs earnings, and preps you for interviews with voice practice.
  4. The application locker — your screener answers, saved once, copied in two clicks whenever you apply.
  5. Tailscale — hosts the dashboard on your Windows PC and makes it securely available on your Mac and iPhone, over real HTTPS, invisible to the public internet.

The compliance line, drawn once: automation points at your own data (your inbox, your notes, your calendar) — never at the platforms. The platforms email you; StudyFlow reads your email. You click "apply" yourself, you answer screeners yourself. That line is what keeps all five accounts alive. (Prolific's 2025–26 authenticity checks detect AI agents at ~100% and AI-written answers at 98.7% — being human at the platform boundary isn't just ethics, it's strategy.)

1 Build the inbox ~15 min

A fresh Gmail used for exactly one thing: research-platform mail. Blast radius if anything leaks: zero.

  1. Create the account at accounts.google.com — something like yourname.research@gmail.com. Use it for nothing else.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification: myaccount.google.com → Security. (Required for the next step.)
  3. Create an App Password: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords → name it studyflow → copy the 16-character password somewhere safe for Step 3. This is how StudyFlow reads the inbox — read-only, revocable any time, no Google Cloud project, no expiring tokens.
  4. As platform mail starts arriving (Step 2), make a filter per platform: open a message → search from:userinterviews.com → "Show search options" → Create filter → Apply label (e.g. "UserInterviews") + Never send to Spam. Repeat for respondent.io, usertesting.com, prolific.com, dscout.com.
  5. Whitelist the two documented invite senders by adding them to Contacts: Schedule@userinterviews.com (study invites) and Compensation@userinterviews.com (payouts).

Why an app password and not "Sign in with Google"? Google's OAuth route for reading Gmail requires a Cloud project, a consent screen, and — for personal apps left in "Testing" mode — refresh tokens that die every 7 days. The app password does the same job in two minutes and never expires until you revoke it. It lives only in a file on your own PC.

Never paste the app password into a chat, email, or anything cloud-side — including AI assistants. It goes in exactly one place: the .env file on your machine (Step 3). If it ever leaks, revoke it at myaccount.google.com and mint a new one; nothing else breaks.

2 Tune the five platforms ~90 min, pays for itself forever

Sign up with your new research Gmail. Then spend the time where it actually moves invite rates — profiles and notification settings.

Respondent

$100–$350+ per interview · the high-ticket platform
  • Verify a real work email (company domain — not gmail). This is the single documented unlock for B2B "Industry Professional" studies, the highest-paying tier on any platform.
  • Complete Settings → Professional background, connect LinkedIn.
  • Enable email + SMS notifications.
  • Payouts land 7–10 business days after the researcher confirms.

User Interviews

$50–$100+ median per study
  • Connect LinkedIn/Facebook — identity verification measurably drives matches.
  • Keep job title + location current; use Quick Apply studies.
  • Official warning: AI-written screener answers are spotted and hurt approval. Write your own — the locker makes it fast.
  • Invites come from Schedule@userinterviews.com; reminders re-send after 48h.

UserTesting

~$10 per 20-min test · 1–2 tests/week typical · most speed-sensitive
  • Tests are first-come, first-served. Settings → Notification Preferences → enable Email and Sounds; keep the dashboard tab open during your active hours.
  • Your rating = average of your last 12 reviews and directly controls invite volume. Narrate constantly, follow instructions exactly.
  • Dispute unfair 1–2★ ratings — staff manually review them.

Prolific

$8–13/hr steady volume · AI-annotation tasks pay best · strictest rules
  • Fill out 100% of "About You", especially priority questions. Never "rather not say".
  • Install the official Prolific Assistant extension (Chrome/Firefox) — the only sanctioned instant-alert tool; third-party snipers risk bans.
  • No VPN, ever (even iCloud Private Relay). No rushing — suspiciously fast submissions are themselves a fraud flag.
  • Banned accounts re-apply through a waitlist with ~13% acceptance. Don't test the fence.

dscout

$25–$150 per mission · lumpy bonus income
  • Missions are selfie-video diaries; selection weighs "mission fit + scout quality" — submit well-lit, articulate screener videos.
  • Enable push notifications in the dscout app; applications cap out, so earlier is safer.
  • 10–20% application acceptance is normal — volume of applications wins.

Honest math

What "good" actually looks like
  • An active generalist running all five: ~$200–600/month. Niche professionals (devs, healthcare, finance, founders) with verified work emails can do multiples of that on Respondent/User Interviews alone.
  • User Interviews officially calls 1–2 studies per six months typical for a passive profile — the gap between passive and tuned-up is the whole game, and it's exactly what Steps 1–3 close.

The three account-killers, on every platform: ① AI-generated screener/study answers ② VPNs & location spoofing ③ duplicate accounts or borrowed identities. All five platforms terminate for misrepresentation; Respondent also claws back earned incentives. One account, your real self, your own words.

3 Light up StudyFlow ~10 min

Clone, configure, run. The dashboard auto-detects the server and switches from demo mode to live SQLite + Gmail polling.

  1. Install Node.js 20+ (LTS) if you haven't.
  2. In a terminal (PowerShell on Windows): 
    git clone <your-repo-url> StudyFlow
cd StudyFlow
npm install
copy .env.example .env     # macOS/Linux: cp .env.example .env
  3. Open .env in any editor and fill in two lines with Step 1's credentials: 
    GMAIL_USER=yourname.research@gmail.com
GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD=abcd efgh ijkl mnop
  4. Start it: 
    npm start
    Open http://localhost:4173. The Offers tab should show "Gmail connected · IMAP · polls every 5 min".
  5. Fill the Application Locker (Offers tab, bottom): name, location, occupation, devices, a 20-second intro, your common screener answers. From now on every application is copy-paste-send.
  6. Hit Sync now — any study invites from the last 7 days appear as ranked offer cards: pay, length, effective hourly, one button to open-and-apply, one to track it in the pipeline.

The loop, once it's running

  • Platform emails an invite → StudyFlow's 5-minute poll picks it up → ranked offer card appears.
  • You glance, open the best one, paste locker answers, apply — under two minutes.
  • "Track as applied" → kanban pipeline → click cards to advance → moving to Paid logs the earning automatically.
  • Dashboard shows totals, effective hourly, per-platform performance, and what needs a follow-up.

4 Host on the Windows PC, use it everywhere ~20 min

Yes — Windows hosts it, your Mac and iPhone reach it, and it costs $0. Tailscale's free plan covers 6 users / unlimited personal devices, and Serve keeps it tailnet-only (never public).

On the Windows PC (host)

  1. Install Tailscale from tailscale.com/download/windows, sign in.
  2. In the admin console → DNS: confirm MagicDNS is on, click Enable HTTPS.
  3. Tray icon → Preferences → Run unattended (keeps the tunnel alive when logged out).
  4. Optional: rename the machine to studyflow in admin console → Machines.
  5. Make StudyFlow start at boot — PowerShell as Administrator: 
    git clone https://github.com/jessety/pm2-installer.git
cd pm2-installer
npm run configure
npm run setup
pm2 start C:\path\to\StudyFlow\server.js --name studyflow
pm2 save
  6. Publish it to your tailnet over HTTPS (same admin PowerShell): 
    tailscale serve --bg 4173
    It prints your URL — e.g. https://studyflow.tail1234.ts.net. The --bg config survives reboots; check with tailscale serve status.

On the Mac

  1. Install Tailscale (App Store or tailscale.com), sign in with the same account, toggle on.
  2. Open the https://…ts.net URL. Real Let's Encrypt padlock, no warnings.

On the iPhone

  1. Tailscale iOS app → sign in → accept the VPN profile → same URL in Safari.

Why HTTPS matters here: browsers only allow microphone access (voice commands, practice mode) on secure origins. http://192.168.x.x:4173 from your Mac would silently kill the mic. The Tailscale Serve URL is a genuine HTTPS origin — voice works everywhere.

If something fails: tailscale status on both machines → both online? tailscale serve status on Windows → proxy live? pm2 list → app running? First cert issuance can take ~60 seconds.

5 Drive it with your voice

Tap the mic (or ⌘+Space inside the app) and talk. On Chrome 139+ recognition runs on-device — audio never leaves your machine.

"What should I apply to?" — top-ranked offer, read aloud
"How much have I made?" — totals + effective hourly
"What's next?" — upcoming sessions, stale follow-ups
"Show pipeline" — kanban view
"Add a lead" — opens the entry form
"Start practice" — voice interview rehearsal

Practice mode reads real moderator-style questions out loud, times your spoken answer, and transcribes it so you can hear yourself ramble — the cheapest interview-performance upgrade that exists. Sessions where you're articulate and easy to work with are what get you re-invited.
